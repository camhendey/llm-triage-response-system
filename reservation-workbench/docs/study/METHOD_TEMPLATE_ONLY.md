# Method 1: template-only (synthetic templates)

Synthetic templates written for this study. They are not copies of any restaurant's documents.

## Booking-note format

```
Table:
Dine time:            (start-end)
Status:               Awaiting confirmation by <date> | Confirmed | Cancelled
Auto gratuity:        Disclosed / n/a
Minors:
Accessibility:
Payment:              One bill / Separate bills
Occasion:
Allergies:
Guest:                name / email / phone
```

## Response templates

- **T1 First response, seating found**: "Thank you for your request. We have space for [N] on [date] from [start] to [end] at [table description]. This is not reserved yet; please confirm and let us know [missing details]."
- **T1b First response, large party**: T1 plus "An automatic gratuity of [x]% applies to parties of [y] or more." and, for the maximum party, "Our team will follow up about minimum-spend requirements."
- **T2 Confirmation**: "Your booking for [N] on [date] at [start] is confirmed in our records. [Arrival grace and duration]."
- **T3 Unavailable**: "We do not have suitable seating at [time]. We could offer [checked alternatives]."
- **T4 Change/cancel received**: "We have received your request to [change/cancel]; we will confirm once it is processed."

Check availability by reading the printed [availability sheet](AVAILABILITY_SHEET.md) by hand.
